What is ricing?

Ricing means customizing your Linux desktop so it looks and feels the way you want: themes, icons, fonts, wallpapers and a window manager that fits your workflow. The word comes from car culture, where people modified their cars to make them look faster, and today Linux users use it for desktop customization.

You do not need to be an expert to start:

  • change the wallpaper and the colour theme,
  • try a different icon pack and a new font,
  • later switch to a tiling window manager.

On this website you can compare popular distros, follow a short installation guide and share your own setup with others.
